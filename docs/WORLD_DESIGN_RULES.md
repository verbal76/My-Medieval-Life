register (`docs/requirements/requirements.json`, ids `WDR-001` onward), so none can be lost. Numbers are permanent:
a rule is never renumbered or reused, and new rules continue from 017.

## Rules 017 onward

### 017 Generational Memory and Living History

Owner directive 2026-10-19 (`docs/directives/2026-10-19_memory_refuges_politics.md`, section 40), verbatim:
"Significant actions must be capable of becoming persistent historical events whose consequences can influence future
generations. NPC knowledge must have plausible sources, familial relationships must be understood, and memories must
connect to actual people, resources, properties, and outcomes."

### 018 Protected Ecological Continuity

Same directive, section 40, verbatim: "Wildlife populations must be finite and responsive to hunting and environmental
pressures while protected breeding refuges allow gradual, habitat-dependent recovery through reproduction and dispersal."

### 019 Living Political Agency and Democratic Consequences

Same directive, section 40, verbatim: "Eligible NPCs must cast genuine individual votes based on their knowledge,
relationships, family histories, interests, and assessment of candidates. Political offices must have real
responsibilities and consequences. Elections, governance, and political succession must continue independently of the
player and persist across generations."

The directive asks for this rule to be added or extended without duplicating "the established rule concerning
political authority, social hierarchy, and civic employment" (015). 019 covers voting and democratic consequence; 015's
detailed text awaits recovery, and the two are to be reconciled when it is found.
