# F015/P99 births investigation (B25R2-CONT-01 item 3), 2026-11-12

Question: the F015/P99 confirmation campaigns showed fewer births (856 vs 718 over four seeds). Does F015/P99 (`wip/b25-f015-p99`
`3d515d6`) change the birth rule, or prevent couples from having children?

## Evidence

**1. The birth rule's inputs are not changed by F015/P99.**
- `Life._try_birth` (`game/scripts/core/life.gd`, line 544 at `fbfd6ad`) refuses a birth when
  `w.brain.household_food_days(h) < birth_min_food_days`, and otherwise checks the mother's age, the gap since her last child and a
  daily chance.
- `git diff fbfd6ad 3d515d6` leaves `household_food_days` unchanged.
- F015 adds a separate measure, `household_food_held_days` (food actually held). Only the homestead's food trips and errands use it,
  to decide when a household goes to get food. `life.gd` is not in the diff.

**2. Read-only diagnostic runs**
- Branches:
  - `wip/b25-births-diag-base` `0051477` = `fbfd6ad` + diagnostic.
  - `wip/b25-births-diag-f015` `40f6d01` = `3d515d6` + diagnostic.
- The diagnostic is a `BIRTHDIAG` line every 5 days. It counts adult women and how many are held back by each birth condition.
- Runs: 43124937749 (base) and 43124942058 (F015); seeds 2 and 4; 365 days; both exit 0.

| Seed | Run | Births | Adult women d0 / d180 / d365 | Held back by food (mean per sample) | Held back by no household | Births per "ready" woman-sample |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | base | 278 | 29 / 50 / 88 | 0.0 | 0.0 | 0.542 |
| 2 | F015 | 248 | 29 / 40 / 59 | 0.0 | 0.0 | 0.408 |
| 4 | base | 216 | 26 / 46 / 67 | 0.0 | 0.0 | 0.455 |
| 4 | F015 | 318 | 26 / 48 / 92 | 0.0 | 0.0 | 0.504 |

- The runs diverge from the first sample: on day 0 for seed 2, and in `food_days_ready_mean` for seed 4. That is consistent with F015
  changing food decisions, which changes the RNG draw sequence. It does not show a changed birth rule.
- In 292 samples, no woman was ever held back by food or by having no household, in either code.
- Births follow the number of adult women. The birth rate per eligible woman moves in opposite directions on the two seeds
  (lower on seed 2, higher on seed 4).

## Conclusion

- **No birth-rule regression was found.**
- F015/P99 does not alter the birth rule or its food input.
- The birth totals are a population outcome that diverges between runs:
  - here, totals of 494 vs 566 on two seeds (F015 higher);
  - in the earlier four-seed campaigns, 856 vs 718 (F015 lower).
- The direction is not consistent across seeds.
- This is evidence, not proof, that the earlier decline is run-to-run divergence rather than a systematic effect.
- Under WDR-022 a population outcome is measured, not a pass/fail criterion.
- Owner decision needed: whether to merge F015/P99 (the merge was held for this investigation).
