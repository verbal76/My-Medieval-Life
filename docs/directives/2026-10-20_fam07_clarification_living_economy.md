One clarification on FAM-07:

An orphan joining the bandit clan does not automatically mean that child must become a bandit upon reaching adulthood.

The clan becomes their household and social environment. They retain their identity, family history, relationships, and developmental progression.

When they reach adulthood, their circumstances, relationships, opportunities, needs, and personal decisions should determine their future. Remaining with the bandits is one possible outcome, not a mandatory one.

For now, implement the simplest compatible behavior without prematurely building the full NPC Judgment or bandit-faction systems. Record any advanced behavior as a future requirement.

Also, preserve the Living Economy Expansion as a separate major build. Its needs-driven economic architecture must eventually integrate with households, employment, production, construction, markets, caravans, mining, the manor, bandits, and NPC decision-making.

Do not delay Build 16 for that overhaul.

Continue working autonomously through the approved Build 16 requirements, fixing genuine defects and documenting legitimate simulation outcomes without trying to force every village into prosperity.
