MML — X20-TRADER-CLOSE OWNER DECISION

Approve reopening settlement trading businesses when a household takes up the trade again.

A settlement business with zero workers must close and stop all trading activity. However, closure does not permanently eliminate that trade from the settlement.

When a household enters the appropriate trade, the business may reopen or be reestablished through the existing business-creation and ownership rules.

Reopening must not restore or duplicate the former owner's money, stock, contracts, workforce, or other assets. Any legitimate outstanding obligations must remain governed by the existing ownership, estate, and transaction rules.

This applies consistently to settlement businesses, not just merchants, wherever the existing simulation permits the trade to be established.

Preserve deterministic behavior and exact conservation of coins and goods.

Include a focused regression test demonstrating that an unstaffed trader closes, remains inactive while unstaffed, and can resume legitimate trade after a new household enters the profession.

Do not remove the Finding 1 fix.

Continue the existing 38-suite validation and merge only after the applicable tests pass. Reuse existing evidence and avoid redundant simulation campaigns.
