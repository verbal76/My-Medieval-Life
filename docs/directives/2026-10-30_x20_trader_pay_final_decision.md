MY MEDIEVAL LIFE — X20-TRADER-PAY FINAL OWNER DECISION

Decision: APPROVED — OPTION A

Continue with the existing trader compensation and reserve rules for the current Expansion 20 integration and next private playtest.

1. Trader compensation

Retain the existing rule requiring traders to maintain 85% of their starting funds as a reserve before distributing excess funds to their household.

Do not introduce minimum wages, revenue-sharing percentages, subsidies, altered reserves, or other economic balance adjustments without my explicit approval.

The fact that a trader household may abandon an unprofitable occupation is legitimate simulation behavior. We are not guaranteeing that every business succeeds.

2. Trader reopening

Worker B's trader reopening implementation at "3702566" is approved in principle.

An unstaffed trader must close and cease operations. A qualifying household may reopen it using its own funds under the existing ownership rules.

The former owner's coins, goods, contracts, obligations and workforce must not reappear.

Preserve the same reopening behavior for the other 11 settlement business types.

Allow the existing baseline simulation on "8bbdcfb" to finish before merging the reopening branch.

Afterward, integrate the branch and run the planned settlement regression suites against the combined code.

Reuse the existing two-seed, 60-day simulation evidence. Do not duplicate those simulations without a demonstrated reason.

3. Deferred investigation — X20-TRADER-PAY

Keep X20-TRADER-PAY open as a deferred economic-balance investigation.

During existing Living Economy work, investigate the actual causes of trader household instability:

- Sales volume and profitability.
- Business reserves and available operating capital.
- Household income and expenses.
- The 85% reserve threshold.
- Occupational switching decisions.
- The effects of migration, business closure and reopening.

Distinguish genuine economic failures from accounting defects or simulation artifacts.

The lower village coin totals reported at day 60 do not, by themselves, establish that money was destroyed. Reconcile coin ownership and distribution before drawing conclusions.

When Living Economy wages and service contracts are ready for consideration, present evidence-based compensation alternatives with their expected consequences.

Do not implement a compensation change until I approve it.

4. Existing development priorities

Continue LE-06 save-growth remediation, multiplayer/economy transaction-integrity work, and SJD-G judgment calibration under their existing instructions and simulation budgets.

Do not enable the Living Economy by default until its blocking issues and required validation are resolved.

Preserve Build 24 and its existing private release.

Do not start additional expensive GitHub Actions campaigns, duplicate completed simulations, or publish another build without authorization.

5. Reporting and autonomy

Record this owner decision in the persistent Expansion 20 decision register and recovery handoff, not solely in temporary scratchpad files.

Continue ordinary implementation, integration, focused testing and documentation without stopping for routine approvals.

Escalate only genuine owner-level decisions, material compatibility risks, unexpected economic behavior requiring a design change, or additional testing outside the approved budget.

Proceed under these decisions.
