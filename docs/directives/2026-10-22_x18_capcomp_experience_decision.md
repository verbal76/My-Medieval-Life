# Owner decision, 2026-10-22: X18-CAPCOMP part 1, workforce experience (verbatim)

Approve R1 as the method for calculating and reporting an industry's workforce capability, with one important clarification.

Individual experience must remain the basis for individual work performance and compensation.

Each worker accumulates industry-specific experience from actual hours worked, using one experience day per eight hours.

Approve the proposed experience caps:

- Mining and forestry: 12 experience days.
- Farming, trade and tanning: 18 experience days.
- Carpentry and smithing: 24 experience days.

Approve the straight-line individual progression from level 0 to level 10 and the one-season activity window for calculating the industry's hours-weighted average.

However, the industry average is a reporting and planning metric, not a modifier automatically applied to every worker.

A level-10 master working alongside a level-0 apprentice must retain their individual productivity advantage. The apprentice must not inherit the master's experience.

Facility limitations may reduce each worker's effective capability, but they must never erase accumulated experience.

Keep the existing hook disabled until the facility rules and starting experience rules are approved.

Record this clarification in X18-CAPCOMP and implement only the approved experience-tracking components on the Expansion 18 branch.

Do not interrupt Build 17 or initiate additional long-duration simulation campaigns.

Build 17's final 365-day simulation remains the immediate priority. Once seed 2 finishes, provide the complete four-seed validation report before advancing additional design questions.
