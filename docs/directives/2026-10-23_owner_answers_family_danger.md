# Owner answers, 2026-10-23: family protection in danger and the family-rule numbers

Answers given in the session. Saved verbatim.

- Danger (FAM-SAC-01 item 4; villagers had no flee-or-defend decision): **"Shield and fetch help (Recommended)"**:
  danger near the family (a bandit, a wolf, a fire): the head moves between it and the dependants, sends them home, and
  runs for a guard or fights back if cornered. They can be hurt or killed doing it. Expansion 19 personality and
  stubbornness bend how readily they stand their ground.
- Family numbers: **"Accept (Recommended)"**: survival floor hunger 25, head's coin floor 3, seek paid work under 20
  coins, self-first exceptions 15% of the stubborn group (about 4.5% of villagers).
