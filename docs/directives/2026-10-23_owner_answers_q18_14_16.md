# Owner answers, 2026-10-23: Q18-14, Q18-15, Q18-16 (couriers; asked one at a time)

| Question | Owner's answer |
|---|---|
| Q18-14: what qualifies a courier to notarise formal contracts carried far away | "New Law skill (Recommended)": add a Law skill, learned by witnessing and carrying documents and taught by the clergy or reeve; a courier needs some Law to notarise |
| Q18-15: which businesses count as "major" and get their own courier booth | "Any business with a shop": every shop or workshop that trades regularly can open its own booth (not the recommendation, which was market traders and the manor) |
| Q18-16: where the village's courier booth stands if no market stall is free | "Build one near the market (Recommended)": the courier builds a small booth on open ground by the market under the normal stall rules (materials and rent) |
