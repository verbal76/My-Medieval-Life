# Owner answers: crime repair decisions (GP-CRIME-PURSUIT)

Owner, 2026-10-24, answering three questions from the root-cause audit
(docs/design/REPAIR_AUDIT_DIALOGUE_CRIME_GUARDS.md). Recorded verbatim as chosen.

1. How should guards spread the alarm about a serious crime?
   Answer: "Add a hue and cry / bell" — a new mechanic: a murder makes guards or villagers raise a shout or
   ring a bell, and everyone within earshot learns of it at once.
2. When a player fights guards and is losing, what can happen besides death?
   Answer: "Yield and be fined" — a beaten player can yield; guards take them in and fine them heavily (and
   take stolen goods back). A known multiple murderer who keeps fighting can still be killed.
3. If a wanted murderer dies, what happens to the case for their heir?
   Answer: "Case ends; stain stays" — guards stop hunting when the offender dies; the family's existing
   reputation carries the stain to the heir.
