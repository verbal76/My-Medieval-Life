F015 — Homesteader starvation despite available coins

Separate actual food inventory from purchasing power. Coins must not count as food already available for household consumption.

Preserve a separate measure of potential food purchasing capacity if the simulation needs it.

When a hungry villager cannot obtain a cooked meal, investigate and implement reasonable fallback purchasing of available edible ingredients or other suitable food, respecting existing cooking, inventory, market, and travel systems.

Do not create food or guarantee that every villager survives. Genuine shortages, isolation, and inability to reach food must remain possible.

Preserve legitimate third-party payment, household support, and existing trade permissions.
