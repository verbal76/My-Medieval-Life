# NPC Judgment: opinion may refuse a transaction, never by a fixed cutoff (owner correction, 2026-10-26)

Received in the session of 2026-10-26, verbatim:

> The statement "opinion may shape a purchase but never block it" is not an accurate representation of my NPC Judgment design. An NPC absolutely may refuse a transaction because of their opinion of another character. However, reputation must not act as a rigid, automatic prohibition based on a numerical threshold. NPCs weigh their opinions against needs, scarcity, available alternatives, prices, relationships, personality and other circumstances. A villager who dislikes someone may still trade with them out of necessity. Another villager in the same circumstances may refuse because their dislike outweighs the benefit. Both outcomes are valid. The Judgment System influences decisions rather than dictating universal outcomes. Preserve that distinction throughout LE-01 and all subsequent Living Economy integration. Do not implement a hard-coded reputation cutoff or a rule requiring every transaction to remain possible regardless of NPC preference. Continue the Build 19 recovery audit and existing approved work without interruption.
