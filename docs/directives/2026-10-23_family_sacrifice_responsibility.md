MY MEDIEVAL LIFE — FAMILY SACRIFICE AND RESPONSIBILITY DIRECTIVE

Add the following owner-approved behavioral rule to the family, household, and NPC decision-making design.

The father figure prioritizes the well-being of his family above his own personal comfort and needs.

The responsibility hierarchy is:

1. Children.
2. Spouse.
3. Parents.
4. Self.

When resources are scarce or family members face hardship, the father figure should willingly sacrifice his own food, comfort, wealth, time, and reasonable personal safety to protect and provide for those above him in this hierarchy.

This must influence actual NPC decisions, including food distribution, resource allocation, employment, spending, caregiving, protection, and responses to danger.

Use existing household relationships, needs, personality, and decision-making infrastructure. Do not create a separate competing family AI system.

Sacrifice must account for long-term family survival. The NPC should not automatically make a suicidal decision when remaining alive would better protect the family.

Record the actual family-priority factors through XEXPL-01 and the simulation flight recorder so we can verify that family responsibilities influence behavior.

Preserve individual personalities and realistic circumstances without allowing ordinary self-interest to routinely override the established family hierarchy.

Integrate this into the appropriate existing expansion workstream. Do not interrupt current recorder development, REC-F1 corrections, or approved expansion work unnecessarily.

Before implementation, identify any genuine ambiguity requiring an owner decision.

## The owner's answers to the four questions raised before implementation (2026-10-23)

| Question | Owner's answer |
|---|---|
| Who is the "father figure"? | "Father and mother alike (Recommended)": each parent or guardian in charge of a household (father, mother, widow, grandparent raising grandchildren) follows the same hierarchy; the father figure is the default head when both parents live |
| Live now or with Expansion 19? | "With Expansion 19 (Recommended)": built into the Expansion 19 decision engine behind its switch; Build 18 behaviour and the determinism baseline unchanged until Expansion 19 is switched on |
| Whom do "children" and "parents" include? | "Everyone in his care (Recommended)": children = his own, his spouse's and children taken in (grandchildren, orphans) in his household; parents = his own and his spouse's parents |
| How far may personality bend the rule? | "Rare exceptions (Recommended)": personality changes how much and how early, never the order; only an extreme, uncommon temperament occasionally puts self first, and that is recorded |
