OWNER DECISION — EXPANSION 19 RELEASE SCOPE

Expansion 19 must ship with its complete approved feature set.

Do NOT release Build 19 with personality or family-reputation layers disabled. Both must be enabled, integrated, and actively influencing NPC decisions.

Do NOT defer unfinished core Expansion 19 functionality to a 19.x update.

This includes subjective judgment, personality, relationships, family reputation, gossip, memory, deliberate lies, inherited family stories, stubbornness, and the approved family-sacrifice hierarchy.

Resolve remaining formulas and implementation decisions before the release gate. Use existing approved owner decisions wherever available and bring genuine unresolved choices to me together with recommendations.

Preserve the existing autonomous simulation architecture, permanent identities, economic conservation, and determinism requirements.

The flight recorder remains available for private development and testing but disabled in public builds.

Complete targeted integration testing and present the finished Build 19 for release authorization. Do not publish automatically.

The four-game-year mayoral term is a settled Expansion 21 decision and must not be reopened.

Continue authorized parallel work without allowing later expansions to delay Build 19 unnecessarily.
