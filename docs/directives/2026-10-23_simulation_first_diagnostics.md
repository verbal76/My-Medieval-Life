MY MEDIEVAL LIFE — SIMULATION-FIRST DIAGNOSTIC DIRECTIVE

Owner authorization: Begin using the flight recorder for autonomous simulation analysis now.

Do not wait for the Excel exporter. The recorder's raw archives are already readable and have demonstrated their value by identifying REC-F1.

Priorities:

1. Continue the already-approved REC-F1 investigation and correction. Do not restart or duplicate that work.
2. Complete the essential recorder instrumentation and permanent-identity integration as already planned.
3. Begin progressively longer autonomous village simulations using the existing raw diagnostic archives.
4. Analyze NPC decisions, failed actions, economic activity, inventories, conservation, relationships, population changes, and other systems where recording coverage exists.
5. Look for repetitive behavior, stalled activity, resource bottlenecks, contradictions, unexplained state changes, and long-term instability.
6. Trace suspicious outcomes back through recorded decisions and interactions to determine actual causes.
7. Distinguish genuine defects from legitimate emergent behavior. Report missing instrumentation rather than assuming unrecorded systems are functioning correctly.

Execution rules:

- Start with short runs and increase duration when the results justify it. Do not launch an enormous simulation campaign automatically.
- Reuse existing simulation results and test evidence.
- Analyze findings between runs rather than repeatedly generating data without examining it.
- Prioritize high-impact defects affecting gameplay, economy, population, or performance.
- Report findings with affected systems, supporting evidence, likely root causes, and recommended corrections.
- Do not automatically implement unrelated gameplay fixes without owner authorization.
- Keep simulation testing separate from the released Build 18 baseline.
- Continue authorized Expansion 19 development and parallel work without unnecessary interruption.
- Defer Excel formatting, dashboards, and advanced export features. Do not discard the existing exporter design.

Important: The recorder is a development instrument, not a new expansion. Its purpose is to accelerate the completion and verification of My Medieval Life, not delay Build 19.

Begin using the recorder's existing capabilities immediately wherever possible. Do not wait for perfect coverage.
