# Owner decisions, 2026-10-23: regional economy (REG-WAGE-D1, REG-BUY-D1, REG-CIRC-D1) and the Build 18 packaging gate

Saved verbatim.

Build 18 owner decisions:

REG-WAGE-D1: APPROVED.

Merge the tested wage-reserve correction from wip/b18-wagefix into Build 18. The reserve exists to protect essential needs, not to prevent hungry worker households from receiving wages when their employer has available funds. Preserve conservation and the legitimate Higham trade deficit.

REG-BUY-D1: APPROVED IN PRINCIPLE.

Settlements may sell food to the village and other settlements. Their willingness and quantity offered should account for household consumption, reserves, expected production, and economic necessity.

Do not impose an absolute prohibition on selling needed food. Settlements must retain economic agency and may make unfavorable decisions when circumstances justify them.

Use existing NPC economic decision mechanisms where applicable. Present any major new selling-policy rules for approval before implementing them.

REG-CIRC-D1: DEFERRED.

Do not artificially introduce finished goods or additional money circulation merely to prevent settlement decline.

First complete the approved shared trading interface, reciprocal purchasing, couriers, and settlement demographics. Then evaluate whether the regional economy has genuine circulation failures.

Continue the demographics and trading-interface workers without interruption.

Preserve the observed economic outcomes as baseline evidence. Do not tune prices, starting money, wages, or settlement production merely to make the numbers look healthier.

Apply MSW-01. Use targeted validation and existing test evidence.

Remember the Build 18 packaging gate: do not create a Build 18 test package until every item on its punch list is closed or explicitly accepted as permanently blocked. No early or partial builds.

A village is allowed to fail. The simulation is not.
