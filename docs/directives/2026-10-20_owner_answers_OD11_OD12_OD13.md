OD-13 — APPROVED: Both bed sharing and house expansion.

Young children may share beds, with reasonable age-based limits. Couples may share beds. Existing village houses must be eligible for living-quarters expansions even if they were generated without land parcels.

Expansions must consume actual resources, labor, and construction time. Do not solve the problem by generating free beds or houses.

OD-11 — APPROVED: Unplaced orphans join the bandit clan.

Follow the previously established guardianship order:

1. Surviving parent.
2. Living grandparents.
3. Nearest eligible blood relative.
4. Another villager's household willing and able to take the child.

If no suitable household can take the child, the child joins the bandit clan.

This is the intended fallback, not an indefinite temporary-care state and not a formal orphanage or adoption process.

Preserve the child's identity, age, genealogy, and existing relationships. Joining the bandit clan does not instantly make a child an adult or grant adult combat abilities.

Integrate this with the planned bandit-faction system. If bandit clans are not yet implemented, document the dependency and create a safe interim handling mechanism without inventing a fully functioning bandit system inside Build 15.

OD-12 — APPROVED: Choose the best-suited equally close relative.

When multiple eligible blood relatives are equally close, prioritize:

1. Ability to provide care.
2. Available household capacity.
3. Access to food and necessities.
4. Existing relationship with the child.
5. Geographic proximity.

Use a deterministic tie-breaker when candidates remain equal.

Blood relationship determines the order of consideration. Practical suitability determines the preferred guardian among equally close relatives.
