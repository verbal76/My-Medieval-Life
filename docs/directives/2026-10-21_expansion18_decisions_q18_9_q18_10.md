Q18-9 — Settlement capability levels

Capability levels affect pay scales and production output only. They must never function as permission gates or prevent an NPC, player, or settlement from performing an activity.

For example, a settlement with mining capability level 0 can still mine if the necessary resources, tools, and access are available. Its capability level influences compensation and productivity.

Q18-10 — Regional purchasing and transportation

Anyone may purchase goods from another settlement, including individual villagers, households, merchants, businesses, and settlement treasuries.

The buyer has two options:

1. Travel to the selling settlement, collect the purchased goods, and transport them personally.
2. Negotiate and pay a willing carter or delivery service to transport the goods.

There is no automatic free delivery, merchant-only purchasing restriction, or artificial creation of coins or goods.

Use the existing NPC negotiation, contract, inventory, transportation, and economic conservation systems wherever applicable. Do not create duplicate systems.
