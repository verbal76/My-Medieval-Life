# Owner decision, 2026-10-21: STONE-Q Part 2 (verbatim)

OWNER DECISION — STONE-Q PART 2

Skill-Based Stone Dressing and Material Conservation

Decision: Option C approved.

The owner approves skill-based stone dressing efficiency as part of Expansion 18.

1. Production and skill

- Raw stone is converted into finished building blocks using the existing STONE-01 stoneworking system.
- Stone dressing efficiency depends on the actual skill of the person performing the work.
- Less experienced workers produce more waste.
- More experienced workers recover a greater proportion of usable building material.
- The same mechanics apply to players, independent homeowners, ordinary NPCs, and professional stonecutters.
- Occupation titles do not grant automatic production bonuses.

Use the existing character skill and capability systems rather than introducing a duplicate skill system.

2. Physical conservation

All input materials must be accounted for.

Stone that does not become a finished block must remain represented as physically conserved waste, chips, or spoil through the appropriate existing material system.

Do not silently destroy raw stone or create finished blocks without corresponding inputs.

Waste must not be converted back into finished blocks without an actual approved recovery process.

Do not introduce an unlimited recycling loop.

3. Production ratios

The owner has not approved specific conversion ratios or skill thresholds.

Do not invent permanent production ratios, efficiency percentages, or progression tables.

During Expansion 18 design, present a proposed skill-to-efficiency model with its effects on resource depletion, construction costs, production economics, and NPC viability.

Obtain owner approval before locking those values into the design.

4. Build 17 scope protection

Build 17 retains its existing 1:1 stone-to-block conversion.

Do not modify the Build 17 recipe, introduce new material losses, change save compatibility, or add release blockers because of this decision.

The future skill-based efficiency system is assigned to Expansion 18 alongside:

- X18-STONECUT: professional stonecutter trade.
- STONE-02: NPC personal-use stone dressing decisions.
- STONE-03: differentiated structural repairs and mixed-material construction.

Integrate these requirements with the existing economic, capability, and production systems when their dependencies are ready.

5. Documentation and validation

Record STONE-Q Part 2 as answered in the requirements register, owner decision log, and relevant stoneworking and Expansion 18 design documents.

Follow WIKI-01. Do not describe skill-based stone loss as currently playable until it has actually been implemented and verified.

When implemented, validate material conservation, deterministic outcomes, player/NPC parity, and the economic consequences of stoneworking efficiency.

Do not rerun comprehensive tests merely to record this decision.

6. Continue to the next owner question

Confirm the decision has been recorded and that Build 17 remains unaffected.

Then present STONE-Q Part 3 exactly as documented in "docs/build17/STONE_01_BLOCKS.md", including:

- The original question.
- Available choices.
- Gameplay and economic consequences.
- Your recommendation.

Do not implement an unanswered decision or interrupt existing workers.
