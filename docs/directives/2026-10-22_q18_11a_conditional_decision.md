# Owner decision, 2026-10-22: Q18-11A, conditional approval of Option A (verbatim)

Q18-11A — Conditional Approval of Option A

I approve the bounded capability model in Option A for mining, forestry, trade, tanning, carpentry, and smithing, using the proposed output and pay modifiers.

However, I want farming handled differently.

A farmer's experience must matter. Experience cannot increase the amount of crops a field produces, but it should improve the farmer's efficiency in completing agricultural work.

For farming:

1. Keep crop yield determined by the land and existing agricultural mechanics.
2. Allow experience to reduce the labor time required for farming activities.
3. Preserve all existing crop growth, harvest, and resource conservation rules.
4. Allow experienced farmers to receive increased compensation consistent with their improved labor efficiency.
5. Do not introduce additional crops or materials through capability multipliers.

Present a proposed farming efficiency and compensation progression across levels 0–10 using the same bounded philosophy as Option A.

Do not implement the farming progression until I approve its numbers.

Preserve the existing distinction between accumulated workforce experience and facility-limited effective capability.

Keep Build 17 unchanged. Continue Expansion 19's gossip optimization independently, and do not interrupt the four ongoing 365-day validation runs.

Do not launch new long-duration simulation campaigns for this documentation decision. Once the complete profile is approved and implemented, identify the minimum necessary validation using existing evidence wherever applicable.
