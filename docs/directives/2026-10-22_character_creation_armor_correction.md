MY MEDIEVAL LIFE — CHARACTER CREATION AND ARMOR DESIGN CORRECTION

OWNER CLARIFICATION

The player must begin as an ordinary villager, not an armored adventurer.

Character creation

Integrate the existing licensed random character creator from our repository into the game's introduction, replacing the eight preset characters.

Character creation is for personal appearance only.

Allow selection or randomization of supported visual features and ordinary civilian clothing.

Do not offer armor, weapons, equipment upgrades, or combat classes as starting character customization options.

Preserve the game's existing approved starting inventory and economic balance.

Armor acquisition

Armor is earned or purchased during gameplay.

Use the existing armor sprite sheets for the equipment's appearance.

Armor must exist as physical, owned inventory, not as an unrestricted cosmetic selection.

Players should be able to visit an armorer to inspect, purchase and equip available armor through the normal economy.

Armor availability must depend on actual stock and production, consistent with the game's simulation rules.

Armor classification and statistics

Inspect the existing armor sprite sheets and any associated metadata.

Identify all armor variants and their intended classes or categories.

Design a corresponding equipment system supporting, where appropriate:

- Armor class or protection rating
- Hit points or durability
- Rarity
- Weight
- Movement effects
- Material and crafting requirements
- Price and availability

Do not invent specific numerical values or assume that sprite artwork contains gameplay statistics.

Present the discovered assets, existing metadata, proposed classifications and any required owner decisions before assigning balance values.

Equipping armor must update the character's visible sprites and appropriate gameplay statistics.

Barber shop

Provide an in-world barber service for changing hairstyles using the existing compatible character assets.

The barber is a normal economic participant. Do not invent service prices or resource rules without owner approval.

Engineering boundaries

Do not modify frozen Build 17.

Plan integration into the next approved development scope.

Reuse existing character generation and armor assets. Avoid duplicate systems.

Apply MSW-01. Inspect first, implement only approved mechanics, and use focused validation.

Do not begin substantial implementation until the asset inventory and design dependencies have been reported.
