# Build 18: tester notes

A private playtest build for the owner (not a public release). What is new since Build 17, what to try, and what is known
not to work yet. The in-game wiki (Help) describes every system below as it plays.

## New things to try

- **Music and sound.** Your three songs play by the time of day in the village (Sunrise over the Village in the morning and
  on the title screen, Prosperous Village by day, Misted Ancient at night and underground); Fair Fight plays in a fight and
  Plain Sight out in the countryside. Ambience (birds, crickets, wind, cave wind, dripping galleries) follows the place and
  hour, and your own actions make sounds: footsteps by the ground (soft), swings and hits, coins, chests, doors, equipping,
  quests, level-ups, rest. Settings has Master, Music and Sound-effects sliders. Windows and Linux carry the songs as WAV,
  Android as Ogg.
- **The chapel.** A preacher and a church assistant keep the chapel east of Mill Road. The pious give at the weekly service;
  the offerings pay the clergy, food relief comes from the church first when a household runs short, and what is left of
  the offerings goes as alms to the poorest households. You can give coins or food, ask for food when you have almost no
  coins, and volunteer on the church's field.
- **Five neighbouring settlements.** Wulstead, Netherton, Garton, Higham-on-the-Way and Bourton Abbas are small economies of
  their own: their traders are the caravan, the village buys food and goods from them, people move between them and the
  village for work, land, marriage or hunger, and their trades open and close as demand and workers allow. You cannot visit
  them in person yet.
- **Couriers.** A courier booth sends goods and coins along regular routes or as special deliveries; formal agreements can
  be signed through a courier-notary who has learned the Law.
- **Barter and credit.** Goods-for-goods deals and recorded debts that must be repaid; unpaid debts hurt your standing.
- **Your own business.** A hired attendant now works your stations with real recipes and materials (set production orders
  and a workroom in the stall window), your stall shows what it sells, and you can open a shop in a leased house. Business
  money is kept apart from your purse.
- **Stone.** Dressing takes 3 stone to 2 blocks and 1 rubble (rubble serves where raw stone does, except in dressing); you
  need a mallet and an iron chisel; Masonry skill can turn the rubble into a third block. A stonecutter may set up in trade.
- **Rivalry.** Craftspeople favour what pays best, the manor raises its hands' wage (a little) when places stay empty, and a
  failing merchant may move stall or give up.
- **Also new:** taxes announced in advance with a margin, feasts and luxury imports, armour you can see, a character creator,
  the barber and the tailor (backpack upgrades), an inventory sorted into pools, a map beyond the village, farm crops shared
  by work, the plough horse, Farming, Husbandry, Hunting, Healing, Carpentry, Masonry and Tanning skills, outlaws returning
  after a season, and life in the countryside.

## Saves

The save format is version 19. A Build 17 save loads (checked with a real Build 17 save): the chapel is placed where its
ground is free (on many older villages villagers' land covers it, and that village has no chapel), nothing the save held is
dropped, and the new systems start empty. Export your save first (Settings, Saves) before installing.

## Known limitations

- Not tried on a physical phone, controller or Windows PC; sound has not been heard on a device (footstep loudness,
  fades and phone memory to check).
- In year-long test runs job places stay fixed while the population grows several times over, so many adults end up
  without work; households grow poorer late in the year. These are outcomes of the economy ("A village is allowed to fail.
  The simulation is not."), recorded for later builds. Netherton tends to empty over a year.
- Some sounds and art are unused or stand-in (magic, bows and water sounds have nothing to play on; the caravan wagon is a
  stand-in sprite).
- Settlement circulation and several-generation settlement checks are deferred to a later build.
