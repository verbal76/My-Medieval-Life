A private playtest checkpoint for the owner (owner decision 2026-10-28), **not the finished Expansion 20**: Android, Windows and

- Still **save version 23**. A Build 22 or 23 save loads with everything kept.

  Keep a copy (Settings, Saves, **Copy my save to a file...**) if you may go back.

## New since Build 23

**Swords you can see.** A drawn sword is held in the hand while you walk, swung through the whole attack and brought back to the
carrying pose, in every direction (in front of the body or behind it as you face). Copper and iron swords look different. Guards,
bandits and villagers who fight swing theirs the same way. The blow lands when the sword strikes.

**People move more freely.** Anyone may leave a neighbouring settlement, a trader or the only family working a business included,
and each household decides for itself how long it will put up with hardship. A business nobody is left to work closes and may be
opened again later. Market traders in the village may also leave in a famine, taking their own stock and purse; their stall is let
again.

**Storefront takings stay with the family that earned them.** When a household sells or gives up its house, or moves away, the coins
in its storefront's till go to the household; the next family starts with an empty till. A household that has died out leaves its till
to the manor (this was not happening before: the next family was paid the old one's takings).
