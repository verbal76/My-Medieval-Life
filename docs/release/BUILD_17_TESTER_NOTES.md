# Build 17: tester notes

A private playtest build for the owner (not a public release). What is new since Build 16 (`b3bbb1c`), what to try, and
what is known not to work yet. The experimental farming switches stay **off**. The in-game wiki (Help) describes every
system below as it plays.

## New things to try

- **Mining underground.** The mine yard leads into a gallery and, by shafts, a cave below. Mine seams with a pickaxe; cut
  new passages into the rock with a timber support standing near the face; set up supports, lay track and push a minecart;
  sink a shaft with a ladder. Worked passages are weighed at dawn: unsupported spans can fall, timber wears and needs
  repairing with a mallet and a plank, and anyone caught in a fall lives or dies (no injuries yet) or is trapped until the
  rubble is dug out with a shovel. You can order a passage backfilled with spoil.
- **Prospecting.** Rust-red, black or green stains and glittering flecks on open ground hint at ore below. Take rock
  samples with a prospector's kit and have a smith assay them; dig a trial pit with a shovel to find out for certain.
  Every deposit is finite.
- **Open pits and quarrying.** Mark out an open pit on common ground with a shovel or pickaxe: earth (topsoil, dirt, spoil)
  is dug with a shovel, stone with a pickaxe, and the earth goes on its spoil heaps. You may not dig on land a household
  holds or on the manor's fields. Worked-out seams leave grey rock to quarry.
- **Shovels and building blocks.** Shovels are made from a smith's blade and a carpenter's haft. Raw stone is dressed into
  building blocks at a workbench with a mallet (2 stone to 2 blocks); houses, workshops and rooms now take blocks, while
  ovens, stoves, forges, the farm gate and the manor's mending still take raw stone.
- **Contracts.** Order goods or hire a villager's work (dig your pit, survey a stain) through the talk window; bigger
  agreements are signed face to face.
- **Mining equipment and the manor.** Carpenters and smiths make supports, ladders, mallets and minecarts; the manor buys
  what its works need and posts shoring jobs. The manor's job board is shared: whoever takes a job first has it, nobody
  holds two, and when no manor hand is free the reeve carries waiting deliveries.
- **Goods that spoil and wear, and ways through a cliff.**

## Changed behaviour you may notice

- The old "work the mine" shift is retired: miners earn by selling ore and stone or by contracts.
- A new player starts as before (20 coins, a rusty pickaxe and a rusty sword); an axe is earned, for example by mining and
  selling stone or ore.
- Builders and labourers buy and sell building blocks; labourers dress stone when blocks are wanted.

## Saves

The save format is version 17. A Build 16 save loads (checked with a real Build 16 save): no deposits are lost and the new
underground, pits, contracts and prospecting start empty. Export your save first (Settings, Saves) before installing.

## Known limitations

- Not tried on a physical phone, controller or Windows PC.
- Some new art is stand-in: the shovel-blade part and the timber support icons read poorly; the cave and gallery tiles are
  placeholder-grade.
- No injuries, illness or treatment yet (a collapse is survive or die); villagers never lay track or sink shafts on their
  own; nobody rescues a trapped villager.
- In year-long test runs some villages are poor (missed meals, unemployment) and chickens can die out: legitimate outcomes
  of the economy ("A village is allowed to fail. The simulation is not."), recorded for later builds.
- No audio.
