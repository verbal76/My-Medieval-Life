## Hands-on testing checklist

What you can actually try in this build. Controls: tap (touch), click (mouse) or press **A** / **E**
next to someone or something to use it. Menu > Controls lists every control.

**Coins first.** You start with 20 coins, and land costs 60 or more, so earn some before testing
property: ask a farmer, woodcutter, miner or the reeve **"Any work for me?"** (the reeve pays from
the manor's chest when it can spare it), do side quests (markers over villagers' heads), or sell
goods with **Trade with …**. The journal's Quests page says who buys what.

1. **Land purchase.** Talk to the reeve at the manor and choose **Land and houses**: buy or lease
   ground or an empty house; **Make it home** picks which house is your home. An empty house can
   also be bought or leased at its own door. Selling back and ending a lease are on the same screen.
2. **House placement.** Menu > **Build** (or **H**): choose a house; a footprint follows your tap or
   mouse (keys or stick move it a tile). Green shows the price and rent; red says why not. **Place
   here**, then **Buy the ground and mark out** or **Lease and mark out**. Workshops (smithy,
   carpenter's shop, bakehouse) unlock once your family owns a house.
3. **Construction.** Use the frame and choose **Deliver what I carry** (planks, stone, logs; a
   workshop also iron bars). The bar over the frame and in the window shows progress. **Give up the
   site** returns everything. To get help, talk to someone out of work and choose **Hire to carry
   building materials**: they carry from your chest, or buy the rest with your coins.
4. **Shops.** Use the tavern counter or a market stall (or its keeper) and the shop opens:
   **Trade with …** (buy and sell). **Talk to …** opens the conversation.
5. **Theft.** Prompts say when something is theft: food on the tavern tables, crates and barrels,
   an unattended stall. **Pick pocket — theft** is in the conversation when you stand close. Anyone
   awake who sees it remembers; guards fine you. Try selling stolen goods back to their owner: they
   recognise them, even after a night in your chest.
6. **Interiors.** Walk in through any door: the roof fades and you see the room. Stand behind a
   roof's side wall: it fades only part way. At night villagers sleep in their beds; use a sleeper
   to **Wake** them, **Pick pocket while asleep**, or leave them. Hitting someone wakes the room.
7. **Inventory.** **Pack** (or **I**): tabs for food, materials, tools, weapons and armour, plus
   Skills and Journal; six gear slots (head, amulet, body, hand, tool, ring). Pick an item to eat,
   wear, take off or drop. Helmets, rings and an amulet are made at the workbench and anvil.
8. **A whole life** (before each release, DVW-A14). Play one character from the start: find work,
   befriend and court someone, marry, set up a household and a home, and carry on as their heir.
   Note where the life stalled or felt empty.

**Please report:** anything cut off on your screen, controls that do nothing, prices that feel
wrong (all are provisional), and where you got stuck.

**Not yet checked on real hardware:** Windows, Android phones and gamepads were only tested on this
computer with simulated input; this build is the first chance to try them for real.
