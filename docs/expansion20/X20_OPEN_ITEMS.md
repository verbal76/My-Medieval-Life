  unfinished Build 19 items, alongside the existing **Living Economy** foundations (kept intact, disabled, formulas unchanged).
- The Build 22 mini-build **delivery** of the five deferred work areas is kept (`docs/backlog/BUILD22_MINI_BUILD.md`); they are
  planned under the Expansion 20 umbrella.
- Business desk and courier are tracked separately (`GP-BUSINESS-DESK`, `GP-BUSINESS-COURIER`); their unfinished, untested work is on
  `wip/build22-business-desk` at `e25eec9` and must never be discarded, overwritten, restarted or marked complete.
- Tables preview merchandise only (no pickup). Player-children rules as approved; the adoption agency is a separate deferred design item
  (`MP-ADOPTION-AGENCY`).
- Completed and accepted Build 19 work is not reopened; the Build 19 reputation system is not redesigned or weakened.

## Numbering note (resolved conflict)

Release numbers and expansion numbers have diverged: **Builds 20 and 21** were releases of the multiplayer and polish branch
(`wip/multiplayer-b19`), and the **Build 22 mini-build** is a release, while the register's `builds` map numbers **expansions**
(20 Living Economy, 21 Village Politics, 22 Settlement Advancement). On 2026-10-24 the five deferred items had been given target `22`,
which in the register means Expansion 22; they now carry target `20` with their Build 22 mini-build delivery in `notes`.
