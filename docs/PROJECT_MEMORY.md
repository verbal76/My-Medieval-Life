## What the project is

My Medieval Life (Hot Attic Games, repository `verbal76/My-Medieval-Life`) is a 2D pixel-art medieval life simulation, built in Godot 4.7 (`game/`). It ships to Android first, then Windows and Linux, from one commit with one build number.

A village of about a hundred people works, trades, eats, courts, ages, builds and dies whether or not the player is present. The world must keep living on its own, and the player is the variable that changes its history, not what keeps it alive.

The governing principles are the owner's numbered World Design Rules in `docs/WORLD_DESIGN_RULES.md`. Rules 001–016 are verbatim from the owner's PDF in `docs/directives/`; 017 onward were added later. Among them:
- finite resources and real consumption;
- material and professional interdependency;
- a living world independent of the player;
- emergent gameplay;
- depth without obscurity;
- automated compliance.

The living-world philosophy is preserved in the nonbinding conceptual `docs/MEDIEVAL_LIVING_WORLD_SYSTEM_DESIGN.md`. Actual game requirements and decisions come from the live register and recent owner directives; `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` is historical.
**Authority (owner, 2026-10-27, `docs/directives/2026-10-27_39_decisions_current_first_reconciliation.md`):** the original game design document, the Game Designer import (`docs/game-designer/`) and `docs/DESIGN_GAP_ASSESSMENT.md` are historical reference only, never primary authority; the latest owner instructions, the register and the implemented, validated game come first. Old-design content marked GDD-REVIEW there (Morris era and atlas, a magic system beyond Fireball, monsters and bosses, a chosen hero, an automatic updater, fixed content quotas) is parked and needs explicit current-game reconfirmation.

**CI reporting.** Every substantial workflow run writes a job summary. `tests.yml` and `requirements.yml` also write
`ci-report.json` (format `mml-ci-report/1`, written by the ci_report.py tool) and per-suite logs as an artifact. They run every
suite even after a failure, and classify the run as TESTED, FAILED or INCOMPLETE, never VALIDATED or RELEASED. The tool and
the workflow changes are on the CI branch docs/hag-memory-rationale-2026-11-02 until the lead merges them into the Build 25
source. (2026-11-09 note: it was merged. `.github/workflows/tests.yml` on `wip/b25r2-rev2` calls ci_report.py.) `long-sims.yml` and the
flight-recorder workflows already wrote summaries.

Artifacts expire, so durable conclusions go into the changelog and known issues, with run links. Automation never commits to
a code branch under test (see [the HAG-MEM-02 decision record](decisions/HAG-MEM-02.md)).

## Open owner decisions and blockers

In the register:
- entries of kind `decision` with status BLOCKED (owner decisions);
- entries whose `roadmap_gate` is `owner decision first`;
- entries with `target_build` `owner decision` or `owner action`.

The ledger's Roadmap section lists them under "Waiting on an owner decision" and "Owner or external action". A decision is resolved only by the owner's words (`owner_answer`), and the validator refuses anything else.

Never answer an open decision on the owner's behalf, and never treat a proposal as approved.
