# The time model (OD-21)

The owner's decision of 2026-10-20 (`docs/directives/2026-10-20_od21_time_model.md`): keep 24-hour game days, 8-day
years and four 2-day seasons, and give every timed system an explicit meaning. Three classifications, not three
clocks:

- **Operational time:** hunger, sleep, the work day, travel, immediate actions. Hours and days; does not scale with
  the calendar.
- **Economic time:** wages, rents, prices, contracts, reserves, production costs. Operational days or calendar periods,
  whichever the obligation is.
- **Calendar time:** seasons, ageing, births, generations, seasonal agriculture, long-term biology. Years and seasons;
  scales with `calendar.year_days`.

Register: OD-21, TIME-01 (this audit), TIME-02 (explicit units and one conversion layer), TIME-03 (retuning proposals,
owner review first).

**Status: the audit is being written** (TIME-01). Nothing below this line is yet a finding.
