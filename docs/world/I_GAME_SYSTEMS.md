# I. Game-system opportunity register

Prioritised systems that the source supports or inspires. For each, the **Evidence** column is
CANON (chapter references; quotes in `research/registries/facts.json`). The **Proposal** column is
**GAME EXPANSION**: our idea, not Morris's, and not approved (CLAUDE.md §11: every gameplay
mechanic needs owner approval). The 175 raw ideas noted while reading are in
`I2_OPPORTUNITY_NOTES.md`.

Priority reflects (a) fit with what the owner has set (an ordinary person's life, not a chosen
one; learnable magic; small player-hosted multiplayer; a world larger than the player) and (b) how
much the novel actually supports it.

## Systems the source argues *against*

- **A fixed central plot for the player.** Ralph's story is history in this world (D §2). The novel
  is built on coincidence and destiny for Ralph, which is the opposite of the owner's "not the
  chosen one" rule. Use its events as world state and rumours, not as a quest line.
- **Uniform currency or prices.** The text never gives a price list or exchange rate. Any economy
  numbers are invented and must be labelled so.
- **Elemental magic as Morris lore.** The novel has none (MAGIC_RESEARCH §1.3).
