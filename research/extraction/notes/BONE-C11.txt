summary: A dark-haired woodsman with a bow meets Ralph, leads him a furlong to a hidden brook to eat, and tells him the Burg is a good town under a good lord while Hampton under Scaur is oppressed by the Fellowship of the Dry Tree from the Castle of the Scaur. He mentions Apthorp, a debateable thorp west of the Burg. Four Dry Tree horsemen chase them; one shoots a Turk bow from horseback. They escape to the highway, and the man names himself Roger of the Rope-walk. Near the Burg they pass a watch-tower (one of twenty-seven around the Frank of the Burg) and tilled land, and see the Burg's walls across meadows.
geo: A furlong from the crossroads, a hollow with a clear stream among alders. Apthorp lies somewhat west of the Burg: open, unfenced, debateable between Burg and Dry Tree. The highway runs to the Burg Gate; the wood thins into tillage near the Burg. Twenty-seven watch-towers ring the outskirts of the Frank of the Burg (one on a knoll in a wheatfield, with thatched houses beneath). Then fields, meadows and homesteads, a tiny thorp on rising ground with a view of the walls, and finally only green meadows with sheep, kine and a winding stream up to the walls.
