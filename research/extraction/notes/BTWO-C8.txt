summary: The Lady and the Teacher, marked by bead necklaces, sought and found the Well after much toil and drank; the Lady became as now and the Teacher about forty. About five years ago the Teacher left for the most ancient parts of the world. A month later three Dry Tree men came: old Geoffrey (the young knight who loved her long ago) and his two sons, last of twenty-one sworn seekers, saying it was fifty years since she vanished. She proved who she was. Geoffrey told her the Dry Tree had built a castle on the Scaur above Hampton, the Burg had grown great, and the Land of the Tower was ruined with grass in its Great City streets. They brought her to the Land of Abundance, where she lived; she bought the book from a monk. Dry Tree men often visited and asked her love.
