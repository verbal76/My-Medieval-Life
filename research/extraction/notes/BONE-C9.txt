summary: Waiting at the crossroads, Ralph sees two armed men like the Burg's company come from the north-west, one carrying a red-haired man's head and the other leading a scantily clad, richly adorned woman by a rope to "her due doom". Ralph kills both; the woman helps him. She warns him that the Burg folk are robbers and murderers who would hang him, advises him to go east to Hampton under Scaur, kisses him and rides away south-east. Ralph sleeps in a hazel and chestnut coppice by the crossroads.
