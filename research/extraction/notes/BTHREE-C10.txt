summary: After a rest day of revision, they cross the plain; at noon on the third day from the rock-sea they reach the foot of the fire-scorched ness. There are no foothills, only a maze of fallen rocks. Rounding the ness, they see on a smooth black cliff a carved warrior about sixty feet tall in ancient mail and helm, holding a sword, with a stern face: the Rock of the Fighting Man. The Sage leaves them there without farewell. They ride into a very narrow pass, twilight by day, like an endless prison, and shelter in a rock cleft. Ralph sinks into dread until Ursula draws him out by asking about Upmeads.
