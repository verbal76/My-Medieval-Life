summary: After five days in Whiteness, Clement hires the lord's men-at-arms and servants; ninety enter the mountains. Ralph arms Bull with sword and shield. Seven days through a lifeless stony maze with dwarf willows; the local kindreds are too feeble to attack. On the seventh evening, going downhill with waters flowing east, Ralph and Bull find Bull Nosy's dried corpse under a cliff, skull cloven through an iron cap. Bull wails and vows to kill the damsel; Ralph, frantic, is tripped and bound. The captain and Clement judge the blow a strong man's with a big sword, not a woman's. Bull buries his brother under a howe and recommits to Ralph. Ralph has a vision of Dorothea alive in a gilded marble chamber.
geo: From Whiteness the Goldburg road crosses seven days of stony, barren mountain maze with dwarf willows. On the seventh day the way goes downhill and waters run east (a second watershed). A water source and cliff lie where Bull Nosy fell.
