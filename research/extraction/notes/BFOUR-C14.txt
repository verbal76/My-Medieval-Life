summary: By moonlight Richard asks whether to take the beaten road by the Swelling Flood ford, or the shorter wildwood way over downs and forest to the Burg of the Four Friths; both Richard and the Sage know the wilds. Rumour says the Burg's old fierceness is broken and it is now peaceful. The Sage says they have lore enough to hoodwink foes. Richard quietly warns that the short way passes the places of the past; Ralph wants to test his heart. They leave the ford road and ride a deep ghyll in the waste so that Blaise's riders will search the ford, and sleep at an empty shepherd's cot with an old dog, bread, cheese and a well.
geo: From Whitwall two routes lead toward the Burg of the Four Friths: down to the ford of the Swelling Flood and the beaten way, or the shorter route over downland and forest. The Burg is "not very far from Whitwall". The shortcut leaves the ford road into a deep ghyll of the waste.
