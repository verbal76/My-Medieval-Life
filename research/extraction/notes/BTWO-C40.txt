summary: Leaving Vale Turris by a good highway past the Tower and over the valley ridge, they see the Great Mountains and a sea of woodland breaking grey against the mountain wall. The road runs downhill and then along the boundary between tillage and fenced pasture on one side and the wildwood, always on their right, on the other. David keeps Ralph from mingling with the Queen's people. Toward evening the Lord gallops past with Otter's men and, beside him on a white palfrey, the yellow-haired woman Ralph took for the Queen. Ralph realises she is not the Queen; David will not discuss it.
