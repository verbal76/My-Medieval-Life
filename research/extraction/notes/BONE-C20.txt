summary: Ralph crosses the river by a ford with stepping stones and at a cot gets milk from an old woman and her niece. The old woman hints that others have waited at the castle before him, that the Lady is long-lived but young and fair, and describes seeing her bathe. Ralph walks south and east into the wood and at noon meets a priest on a belled hackney, the Vicar appointed by the fathers of the Thorn to serve the church of the Little Plain and the chapel of St. Anthony. The priest swears the Lady is a holy woman, describes her going barefoot by night to St. Anthony's chapel, weeps, and says she will love none but saints, save a man all women love.
geo: A wide ford with stepping stones below the bathing pool; a cot across the river near the wood. Ralph goes south and east of the House this day (the day before he went west and north). The Little Plain, Land of Abundance and Forest Land are names for this region. The chapel of St. Anthony lies in the wood. The priest's cell is by the church near the ford.
