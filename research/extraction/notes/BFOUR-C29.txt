summary: Before dawn the host musters in Wulstead's market-place: 1,083 men, made up of Ralph's company, Wulstead's men and the Upmeads refugees (mostly old men and lads). Ursula rides armed with a hauberk and sallet, guarded by the armed Sage. Hugh joins the Champions. Ralph goes afoot with the gold-lettered guisarme and takes off all armour but his sallet. They march downhill to the brow of the little hill above Upmeads soon after sunrise. The meadows are black with Burgers; the High House stands unburned under the fruited-tree banner. Ralph's council chooses to await the attack on the hillside, and the Dry Tree stay horsed to pursue. A white-steeled Burger herald demands the Upmeads "thralls"; Ralph refuses. The High House men hold the stone Bridge. The Burgers attack in two battles. The Dry Tree charge the riders and are swallowed. Ralph strides onto a little mound, raises the guisarme and cries "Home now! Home to Upmeads!"; the Burgers who see him throw down their weapons and flee in terror. The Shepherds cut them down. Ralph and Clement relieve the Champions; the Burger horse flee to the Bridge into arrows and Nicholas's men. About two hundred prisoners are stripped and driven back into the Wood Debateable. Ralph's side loses three Dry Tree champions killed and about twenty hurt, Hugh slightly; no Shepherd is slain. Ursula is shaken; Ralph weeps after battle. Ursula waits at Richard's house by the water.
geo: Wulstead to Upmeads is all downhill, over familiar fields, to the brow of a low little hill above the Upmeads meadows. Upmeads Water runs through the meadows between the hill and the High House. One reach comes near the House and bends nearly a quarter-circle about it before running east down the meadows. At the nearest point is a wide, well-built stone Bridge. The meadows have deep river pools, a mound under an ancient thorn, and a little mound on the hillside. Richard's house is on this side of the water.
