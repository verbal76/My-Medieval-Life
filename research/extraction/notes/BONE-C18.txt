summary: Ralph wakes at noon in the House (Castle) of Abundance; Roger has gone, asking him to wait two days. The old carline flatters him and says the land is ruled by the Lady of Abundance, fairest of women, and that a book in the house tells of the Well. Haymakers say they are free yeomen and vavassors without thralldom, and that the Lady has been the same unchanging fair woman through an old man's whole life; they never go beyond the wood. Ralph explores the empty but fully armed castle and finds the Lady's chamber of estate, whose arras shows a richly dressed old woman and a poorly dressed young woman who looks like the lady he rescued. He hunts a hart; the carline says the Lady may be long in coming, as the way is long. That night she gives him the book.
geo: The castle stands in a plain amid the wildwood (the Plain of Abundance): meadows, tillage, a river winding close to the gate and the north-west corner, a wooden footbridge with a willow-fringed bathing pool below, thatched cots between river and wood, and a small white church. The wasted land with the wide river lies north beyond the wildwood. Ralph hunts west and north of the house.
