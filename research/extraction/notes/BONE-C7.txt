summary: Ralph rides south from Higham through the faubourg, river meadows and a vine-and-orchard country of little hills to the thorp of Bourton Abbas, the Abbey's outermost land. At the tavern of the Nicholas a sad maiden tells him her lover rode toward the Burg through the Wood Perilous five days ago and his horse came back bloody; she begs Ralph to wait for the chapmen's company. She knows hearsay of the Well but will not tell it. They kiss and he rides on.
geo: Outside Higham's gate: a faubourg of houses with gardens and orchards, then meadows along the river, which the straight road crosses three times (one stone bridge, two fords). Then little hills and dales with vineyards, orchards and cornfields hiding Higham. By noon Ralph reaches Bourton Abbas, a thorp of grey stone houses in a more wooded valley. The Wood Perilous lies between Bourton Abbas and the Burg of the Four Friths and all about the Burg; from Bourton Abbas at noon one cannot reach the Burg before nightfall.
