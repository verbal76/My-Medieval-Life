summary: Ralph and Ursula leave the Well, cross the Dry Desert easily back to the House of the Sorceress, where the Innocent Folk see them as like gods and ask them to be kind and lowly. The elder's son and his wife guide them back to the Innocent Folk's town, where they stay two days and refuse to winter, longing for home. Ten gaily dressed men with pack beasts take them to Chestnut Vale and on to the mouth of the pass to the Fighting Man, which the Innocent Folk will not pass. They call the mountains the Wall of Strife, as the westerners call them the Wall of the World. With a pack ox and supplies the two pass the mountains in calm late autumn.
