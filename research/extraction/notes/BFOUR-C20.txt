A knight of the town refuses entry to men bearing the Dry Tree, calling them strong-thieves. Ralph's brother Hugh, a poorly armed man-at-arms sworn to Higham, slips through the barrier and joins him. The knight threatens to sally; Ralph offers to meet them man for man. The hundred Dry Tree riders arrive over the hill and the knight retreats behind the barrier, shooting a bolt and some arrows harmlessly. Ralph will not fight; they skirt the walls out of bowshot and ride by moonlight for the down country, Hugh riding beside Ralph.
