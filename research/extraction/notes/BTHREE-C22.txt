summary: They wake toward evening as the tide returns. Ursula asks about scars as a sign of renewal. Ralph had a sword-scar on the shoulder from a raid on Upmeads at sixteen, when he rode with Nicholas Longshanks against lifters; in a tidal salt pool Ursula finds it gone, and her own scar from a hart's tine five years ago is gone too. They notice the Well drains seaward through three arched openings in the pound's outer wall, which is masterfully built. Ralph dreams of the High House of Upmeads greeting them and is eager for the road; Ursula says his foes already fear him. They walk the green meadows, where deer, thrushes and hares are tame, like the Garden of God, and sleep by the green knoll to the sound of the Well.
