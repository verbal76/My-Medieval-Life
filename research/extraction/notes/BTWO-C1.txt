summary: Before dawn the Lady wakes Ralph and leads him, armed, through the pine wood and across a waste into thick wood to a hazel-ringed clearing with great grey stones like a broken doom-ring. Weeping, she tells him the Knight of the Sun is her husband, who cursed and drove her out before they were bedded, misled by Walter the Black's tale that she was a wise-wife and enchantress. The Knight's brother is the Captain of the Dry Tree, the tall man Ralph has met four times. She sent Roger to bring Ralph to Abundance but was caught by her husband and Walter near the Water of the Oak. She swears she will be good and true to him, and they become lovers.
