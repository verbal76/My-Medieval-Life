summary: Ralph and Roger enter the Burg of the Four Friths by the North Gate. Ralph gives his name as "Ralph Motherson"; the gate captain lets Roger serve Ralph while he stays and invites Ralph into the Burg's host. An armourer refuses to sell Ralph an axe without a weapons licence under the Burg's seal. In the market-place a four-armed gallows labelled THE DRY TREE awaits the captured woodmen; a franklin and Roger speak of the Dry Tree's "great witch" and Queen, who is sometimes fair and sometimes foul. They lodge at the Flower de Luce.
geo: The Burg is walled with white-towered ashlar too high to see the roofs over. The highway runs straight to the master-gate (the North Gate), with a walled bailey in front and a deep clean moat, drawbridge and portcullis. Inside: wide paved streets lined with booths, low stout houses, and a big market-place with the plain master church on one side and the Flower de Luce opposite.
