summary: In the small kingdom of Upmeads, King Peter's four sons ask leave to seek adventure. They draw straws: Hugh goes north, Gregory west, Blaise east, and Ralph, drawing the shortest, must stay home. Each is given a squire, horses and a bag of money, and the three elder brothers ride away.
geo: The High House of Upmeads stands in an ingle of Upmeads Water, four furlongs from the Four-want-way. Land rises north toward hill-country and unseen mountains. A low ridge runs south of the river (which flows west to east), and beyond it higher hills mark the southern end of the kingdom. The northern marches end in a great debateable forest (the Wood Debateable), beyond which a bishop-baron rules. Neighbouring lands named in the coinage: the Wolds and the Overhill-Countries.
