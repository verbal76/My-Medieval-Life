summary: They winter at the Sage's hermitage, since the mountains west of Goldburg and of Cheaping Knowe cannot be crossed in winter. In May the Sage brings them through the wood to Vale Turris in four days. The dale is full of tents, booths, a market and six half-built timber houses; the Bull banner flies from the Tower. Riders bring them to the new Lord on a knoll by the river: Bull Shockhead. He jokes that Ursula, his brother's thrall, is his by heritage, then that as Ralph's thrall he cannot inherit, so both are free. He has cast down the Red, White and Black Pillars and made Utterbol merry; he slew the tyrant for his brother's murder, married the widow in atonement, and knows from Agatha of the trap set for Ralph. Ralph declines to come to Utterbol, wishing to help his own folk. Bull offers a feast, twenty spears to Goldburg, and says Ralph's name and description are known in the mountains beyond Cheaping Knowe, where all will help him. Otter is now Bull's right-hand man; Otter refused the lordship.
