summary: In spring, bears return. Ralph sees that Ursula has left her sword behind, runs to the river, and finds her naked and chased by a huge he-bear; she strikes at it with a little axe, which it knocks away. Ralph spears it in the side and kills it with his sword. They kiss as lovers and pledge themselves. Ursula asks to wait to wed until the messengers of the Innocent Folk come, as they do each spring to the cave, so there will be witnesses as in old custom. She tells Ralph it was seeing him that showed her she did not love her former lover. She says they cannot go home without drinking of the Well, since the homeward road's perils would overcome them. Ralph skins the bear about two furlongs from the house.
geo: A chestnut grove lies between the cave and the river; grass runs down to the water. The bear lay about two furlongs from the dwelling.
