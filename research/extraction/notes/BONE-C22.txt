summary: Going due south from the castle all day, Ralph reaches a pine wood edging a long narrow lake. By three great oaks a huge knight with a golden sun on blue strikes down a knight in black while the lady from the crossroads, now in green and a black cote-hardy with a collar like Dame Katherine's, watches. The Knight of the Sun says he has slain his friend for her; she refuses to go with him. Ralph attacks, is beaten down, and the lady stops the knight's dagger, saying she owes Ralph a life. From her horse Silverfax she bargains: she will go home with the knight to Sunway (she says "Sunhome") if Ralph lives and comes with them. The knight agrees, taking Ralph as his prisoner.
geo: Due south of the Castle of Abundance a day's walk: thickets change, then a great pine wood. Beyond, a green strand and a long smooth lake between green banks; at the east end the bank slopes to the pines, with three great oaks on a sward. The lady says the ways behind her to the Dry Tree are open and that beyond the Gliding River she would meet Roger of the Ropewalk and his men.
