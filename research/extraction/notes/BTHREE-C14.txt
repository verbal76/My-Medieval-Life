summary: Returning, Ralph finds the messengers of the Innocent Folk at the cave: two young men and an elder, brown-skinned, tall and cheerful, in white wool, with green-stone axes and bows, riding oxen with two pack oxen. They will guide the seekers to the House of the Sorceress, where others will help. Asked whether wedding will harm the quest, the elder says no and rejoices. They come yearly by a promise made to the fairest woman of the world when she came back from the Well, and have found no seekers for many years. They build a wattled bridal bower roofed with skins and garlanded, provide meal, wine in hooped kegs, carved wooden cups, and white wool robes embroidered with bright feathers. At moonrise Ralph and Ursula are wed before them as witnesses.
