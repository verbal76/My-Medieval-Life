summary: At two hours after midnight Ralph meets Redhead at the dead oak, takes the horse, and rides into the wood. At sunrise a dishevelled dark woman runs to him crying that her lady, the "Lady of the Burnt Rock", has been taken by Riders of Utterbol. It is Agatha, unscratched despite the briars. She then admits the ruse: her mistress is the Queen of Utterbol, who hoped Ralph would rescue her and come to love her. The Lord agreed, wanting his wife's jealousy diverted from his new beloved damsel, and meant to seize Ralph later. Agatha says Redhead is honest and his warnings true; the Lord loves and even fears the damsel, who has refused him, and Agatha wishes she were gone from Utterbol. Ralph pardons Agatha; she promises to keep the Queen in the woods two or three days, asks a kiss, and says she will suffer for him.
geo: Beyond the highway the wood is not very thick after the first mile, with clearings and wood-lawns. Near the camp is a pleasant oak glade.
