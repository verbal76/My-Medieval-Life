summary: Ralph walks the Burg alone. Its folk are dark, short and plainly but stoutly dressed; tall fair women in short yellow embroidered gowns with an iron ring on the right arm are thralls. Merchants in long gowns and turbans trade in booths. At the South Gate the Burg's host returns with wounded men, cattle, sheep, horses, spoil and women captives only. A carline sings a raiding song. A yellow-gowned damsel takes offence at his question about the captives, and the carline curses her as a thrall. Ralph longs to see the Lady of the Wildwood again.
geo: The Burg has a North and a South Gate facing each other, an East Gate, a wide clear way inside the wall, and wide ways running from the Great Place to each gate. There is no castle: the burghers say the whole Burg is a castle. There is a fish and fowl market. A narrow street runs east from the South Gate.
