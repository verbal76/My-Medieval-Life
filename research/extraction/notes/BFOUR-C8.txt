summary: They pass safely through Morfinn's country to Goldburg, now at firm peace with the new Lord of Utterbol. Ralph hopes the Queen will not send for him. Redhead says the Clerk of the Porte will bring a licence to hire men as far as Cheaping Knowe, and that a King now rules. The host tells Ralph that a little over a year ago in early spring the Queen vanished. Folk say she was of the fairy, or killed herself for love. A fisherman took a cloaked woman with jewelled rings, a gold arm-ring and jewelled gold shoes down the river by night; at sunrise, twenty leagues seaward in a great wood, she leapt ashore into the thicket. Her cousin King Rainald now reigns, wise and mighty and no tyrant. Ralph grieves but thinks it better not to seek her.
