summary: Before dawn Roger wakes Ralph and leads him out of the Burg through a postern between the East and South Gates, where friends have killed the guards; they cross the ditch on a plank, Falcon left behind. The party (the big man, a slender youth and others) are spotted at a grange; the big man shoots two pursuers and cries "The third time!" In the wood south of the Burg they meet more than a score men in white armour and white gaberdines; the rescued prisoners rejoin them. The slender youth is deferred to by all. Ralph chooses to leave with Roger alone and is given a horse; the big man says that will make "the fourth time".
geo: A postern lies in the wall between the East Gate and the South Gate, by a square tower, where the ditch is deep but narrow. Outside: plain fields with a few trees (only here around the Burg), a little wood, hedged meadows, a grange with a barn and sheep-cote, and a wooded slope above it. Beyond lies the thicket that Ralph takes to be part of the Wood Perilous south of the Burg.
