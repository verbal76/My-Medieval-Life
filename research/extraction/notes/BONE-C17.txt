summary: Ralph and Roger ride through stony land to a ridge overlooking a wide, empty, grassy valley with a wide river. They swim it rather than use the usual ford downstream, see armed riders spurring toward the water, and gallop over empty downland that Roger says was laid waste by war, ill hap and sorcery. Through an evil-named wood by moonlight they reach at dawn a plain with meadows, tillage and a little river, and a white castle on a green mound, the house from which folk have sought the Well. A gaunt old woman alone serves them and puts Ralph to bed as master.
