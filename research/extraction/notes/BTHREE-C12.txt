summary: Ralph and Ursula winter busily: both are country-bred and skilled. They love each other but live as friends; Ralph fears her refusal. They rarely wear armour, use the horses to haul sledges, and graze them on wild oats in the sandy bight toward the northern mountains. They see no one. Snow and great winds come, but they can mostly go out to hunt. Ursula tells more of Utterbol's horrors; Ralph vows to kill its Lord after the quest, and Ursula will share his fate. Ursula says she has no home now except her brother. At Candlemas the thaw floods the valley, they hunt waterfowl by raft, and spring arrives with flowers and birds; Ralph longs for Ursula.
geo: At the upper end of the vale's bight, toward the northern mountains, is sandy ground with wild oats. Winter brings snow and great winds from the mountains; the Candlemas thaw floods the valley floor, leaving only the green mound (toft) at the cave mouth dry.
