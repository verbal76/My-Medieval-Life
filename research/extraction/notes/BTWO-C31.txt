summary: Ralph frees Bull Shockhead with gold; Bull makes him a blood-brother, giving a beast-skin purse embroidered with bull's horns holding a knotted grass token, and says he may meet Ralph again in the furthest East. Clement foresees Ralph's return. Morfinn, unarmed with a fiddle and a case of knives, calls himself "Morfinn the Unmanned". He says that a month ago he saw a captive damsel brought into Utterbol, taken from the wild men, and describes her and her torn green gown exactly; the Lord rode beside her. It is early autumn, harvest and vintage. They pass sullen, hungry husbandmen and armed gypsy-like wanderers, leave Goldburg's narrow lands, and cross a big river twice by bridges. At a byway's stone chapel a felon knight with men-at-arms invites them to joust; the let-pass makes him let them go, while Morfinn shakes with fear. At dusk they lodge at an unmarked house among hovels where an old woman speaks a language unknown to Ralph.
geo: East from Goldburg's gate; Goldburg's lands are narrow on this side. The land is fair, rich and almost flat; a big river is crossed twice by bridges and small streams by fords. About two hours before sunset, a crossroads with a byway and an uncouth stone chapel on a rise, near a felon knight's house. At dusk, a cluster of hovels around a better house used as a hostelry.
