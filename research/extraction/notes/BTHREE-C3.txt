summary: In the dark pine wood a rider calls out with a sweet voice, offering food and fellowship, and strikes a fire: a slender figure in local armour with a green surcoat. Ralph realises it is the damsel of Bourton Abbas. She says she is now a runaway thrall from Utterbol, where no shame befell her, and that the Lord is "brought up out of hell". She has the bead necklace from the lovely lady who freed her, who taught her the way and spoke of Ralph. Her name is Ursula, not Dorothy as Ralph's dream said. She knows of the Rock of the Fighting Man and the sage in this wood, now called the Sage of Swevenham, whom they will reach in a day or two, though Utterbol's riders know his dwelling. She will lead Ralph to the Well and says that coming back he shall lead. She watches over him while he sleeps.
geo: The pine wood is very thick with no undergrowth, dark and still, with few animals. The Sage of Swevenham lives in this wood, a day or two from the meeting place; Utterbol's riders know where.
open: none newly; the Ursula/Dorothea question is resolved by narration.
