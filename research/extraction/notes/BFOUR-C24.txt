summary: The Folkmote of the Shepherds gathers in the north-east ingle of Bear Castle. Tall Thomas, an old hoary Elder with a sallet and a guisarme, hallows the mote. Ralph is asked to be their Captain "as a brother", not a hireling; he swears by their fathers and his and takes the ancient gold-lettered guisarme, and the Folk hail him brother. Stephen says the Dry Tree will fight as friends, paid by foes, not by the shepherds whose flocks they once raided. A burly man moves to break up the mote and march. Women bring food. The host is told over: 150 Dry Tree champions (not counting Stephen and Roger), 22 men of Garton and 377 shepherds (about 80 with bows), 554 in all. Giles is armed as a man-at-arms. Ralph goes afoot; Ursula walks beside him. Richard rides ahead with ten champions as scouts.
geo: Bear Castle has earth walls with slopes and a fire on the top, a hallowed place inside, and a dyke outside the rampart with a wide view of lovely blue country.
