summary: At dawn Ursula reveals herself unarmed, then re-arms. She stole her gilded armour, marked with Utterbol's token, from the Lord's nephew, half lover and half tyrant to her. She gives Ralph a short Turk bow. They ride through the pine wood seeking a stream that runs straight down from the high ground where the Sage lives (not from the Great Mountains); the stream Ralph followed turns north toward Utterbol. Men fear the wood, so thieves are few, but Utterbol riders may pursue. She swears by the beads to live as long as they can. She resisted the Lord's demands by threatening to kill him or herself, and came to Utterbol unharmed.
