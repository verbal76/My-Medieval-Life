summary: The Knight of the Sun wishes for a hermit to tend Walter; the lady makes a ring of her fingers and murmurs, and at sunset a Blackfriar hermit rows down the lake, saying the thornbushes spoke and sent him. He names her the Lady of Abundance and says all healing goes from her hands. His hermitage is at the end of the water, and he will later bring Walter to Sunway. The Knight gives Ralph back his arms and begs him to leave; Ralph refuses. They sleep in the open: the lady by the friar and Walter, the Knight between her and the wood, Ralph by the horses.
