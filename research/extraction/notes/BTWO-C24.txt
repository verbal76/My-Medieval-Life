summary: In Cheaping Knowe married women, guarded damsels and concubine slaves may not look at men. On the fourth day Clement takes Ralph to the thrall-market, where a black-bearded merchant in scarlet sells thirteen captives from a stone after buyers inspect them stripped. Privately the merchant says Bull Nosy brought the damsel but she was too proud to sell, refusing to be stripped, so he led her away (she riding, he on foot, since mountain men are no horsemen), probably to Whiteness to give or sell her to the dreaded Lord of Utterbol. Clement calls Utterbol the worst and perhaps mightiest tyrant of these lands.
