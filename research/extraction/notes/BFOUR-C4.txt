summary: The feast is held in the Red Pavilion, Gandolf's gem- and pearl-embroidered tent, now with the Bull in place of the Bear. Ralph sits at the Lord's right and Ursula at his left; Otter arrives in worn arming clothes and greets Ralph. Ralph thinks kindly of the Captain of the Dry Tree and Roger. Otter says the Lord means to grow a cheaping town in the Dale of the Tower and till the wilderness. A golden bull-shaped cup is carried in under a canopy: Bull swears by the Bull to purge Utterbol's lands of thieves and tyrants within three years, "betwixt the mountains and the mark of Goldburg", or die. Otter swears to follow the Bull. Ralph swears to go home to his kindred and refuse help to none on the road. They retire to the Tower to hear how Bull won Utterbol.
geo: Bull plans to purge his lands between the mountains and the mark (border) of Goldburg; the Dale of the Tower is to become a cheaping town, with the wilderness beyond tilled and built on.
