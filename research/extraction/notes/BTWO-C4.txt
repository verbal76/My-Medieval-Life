summary: The Lady continues: one May she bathed in a whitethorn dell far from the house and met a grey old carline with bright eyes, who taught her of the world and her letters in that dell, the Dale of Lore, and told her that to escape she should go a little north of west. On Midsummer Day a young knight with yellow hair, a king's son seeking the Well, met her; they loved each other at once, and he gave her his necklace (like the dead seeker's and Ralph's) as a token. The carline gave her a sharp knife; that evening the witch, seeing the collar, said she had not yet drunk of the Well and rushed at her, and the girl killed her with the knife. She took bread and the dead seeker's necklace, left three great books untouched, and went to the Dale of Lore.
geo: The witch's house lies in "this uttermost wilderness, and the woods before the mountains". The Dale of Lore is a flowery whitethorn dell with a stream, reachable before noon from the house; above it lies a wide plain of waste. The way out to the world of folk is a little north of west.
