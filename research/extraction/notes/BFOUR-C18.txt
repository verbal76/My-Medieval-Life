summary: Emerging from the thicket into the Burg's fields, they see a better life: townsfolk strolling merrily, few weapons, but helms on the walls and watchmen on the South Gate's towers. Stephen a-Hurst, a gate guard and Roger's old comrade, warns that alien riders are reported in Higham's lands and bids them lodge at the Flower de Luce. Roger stays with him. The town is gayer; the women resemble the former thralls but dress less wantonly; the master church is being enlarged; armed men wear the Dry Tree token. At the inn two maidens, one formerly a thrall, say rumour already knows they drank of the Well, and that the town's women helped chase the Burgers and will hold the walls. The Sage foresees war and victory; Ralph agrees.
