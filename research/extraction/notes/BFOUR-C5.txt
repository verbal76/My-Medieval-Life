summary: Bull tells how he won Utterbol. After Ralph left he refused Clement's offer, lived as a fiddler in Goldburg, and tamed an escaped yellow lion in the market (under the great green pillar of the cloister beneath the market-house) by his gift with wild things; he bought it and was paid off by the Porte to leave. With the lion he travelled unmolested to Utterbol, feared as a god-sent marvel. Utterness was poor and wretched; the House of Utterbol is a paradise with hellish life. He performed for the Lady; Agatha warned him. He learned the Lord blamed the Lady for Ursula's flight and tortured Agatha, and that the Lord had killed Bull Nosy. When the Lord returned and called him up, Bull, sword in hand, clove his skull. Otter offered him lordship or death; he was raised on a shield as "the Bull of Utterbol, Lord of the Waste and the Wildwood, and the Mountain-side", married the widow in three days, and freed and endowed Agatha. Otter says he has already raised Utterbol "from Hell to Earth".
geo: Goldburg's market-house has a cloister with a great green pillar. The road from Goldburg to Utterbol runs through Utterness, which is poor. The House of Utterbol has fair gardens, orchards, acres and meadows.
