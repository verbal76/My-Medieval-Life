In her pavilion the Lady of Utterbol confides in her sly thrall confidante Agatha that she suddenly loves Ralph and wants his love. The Lady confesses she is coarse, hot-tempered and unfaithful, and hopes Ralph might pity her. Agatha promises a plan if the Lady obeys her: the Lord must grant Agatha immunity, the Lady must stay away from the joust, and the tall thrall Joyce shall sit in her place. Agatha flirts with the Lord and gets his promise. The Lady threatens punishment at the White Pillar at Utterbol.
