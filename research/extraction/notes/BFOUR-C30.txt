summary: Ralph rides back to Wulstead. Katherine, tearful with bittersweet love, promises to come to Upmeads and foresees more perils and deeds for him. At the Priory gate Ralph promises the townsfolk peace. King Peter and the Dame are ready to leave; the old Prior, Peter's ancient friend, blesses Ralph and asks to hear his tale; Ralph offers him the High House to stay in. Peter remarks that his day is done. They reach the plain field of Upmeads two hours before sunset, going round by the ford to avoid the corpses, which the Upmeads carles are already burying in trenches. At the garth gate Ralph sends his parents in to the high-seat and rides half a mile to Richard's house to fetch Ursula. Nicholas welcomes the old couple; Upmeads folk lodge much of the host in their homes.
