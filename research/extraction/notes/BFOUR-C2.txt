summary: In late autumn they leave the Gates of the Mountains at the Fighting Man. On the plain between the mountains and the lava river the Sage of Swevenham waits in a bough-and-rush bower by a blackthorn copse, having waited since spring. He will go back to Swevenham; Ralph invites him to Upmeads. They cross the lava-sea and see no earth-fires; the Sage says they stopped late last year and that the Giant's Candle foretold a change of masters at Utterbol. In March a knight on a red roan, with a white bull on green and a gold coronet, black-haired and blue-eyed, gave the Sage a safe-conduct sealed with the Bull and said he was Lord of Utterbol by conquest, having slain the old lord, "heathen though I be". Wanderers confirm Utterbol is now merry. Ralph suspects Bull Shockhead. Ursula says no one would have avenged Gandolf of the Bear.
