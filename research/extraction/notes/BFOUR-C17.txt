summary: At sunrise near Abundance, Ralph finds Roger of the Ropewalk, now the "hermit", shaggy, staffed, in russet, living in a rough reed-thatched one-room hut by a stream about a mile away, grieving the Lady. He feeds them and hears Ralph's tale. He says the Lady had him draw Ralph to the Burg and then Abundance, and that Ralph's luck took hers. He tells how the Burg fell: grieving Dry Tree riders ambushed a Burg host bringing Wheat-wearer captives, freed the women and armed them; the women helped rout a second Burg host; the Wheat-wearers rose; with Dry Tree help they beat the Burg's best and then stormed the Burg, whose men fled at the push. The Burgers were expelled, unarmed, all ages, to roam as wolves. The Fellowship of the Dry Tree is scattered: Hampton holds only a few; many live in the Burg with Wheat-wearer wives; the Burg is now a mixed folk under a Dry Tree captain, well defended. Roger agrees to come, though he resents Ursula as having "gotten my Lady's luck"; the Sage says the Lady ordained Ursula as Ralph's mate. They camp beyond the Swelling Flood.
geo: Roger's hut is in a stream lawn about a mile through thickets from their camp, a little north-west of the Plain of Abundance. The Wheat-wearers' land is a long road beyond the Burg's range. The company crosses the Swelling Flood the same day.
