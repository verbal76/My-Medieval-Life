summary: They cross sheep downs with deep clefts, few poor houses and taciturn shepherds. At the noon halt Ralph realises the shortest way to the Burg passes the Castle of Abundance and the scene of the Lady's death; Ursula weeps and he comforts her. Ralph wishes to meet the tall Dry Tree champion again; the Sage warns of grief. About an hour later they reach the stream from the Lady's bathing pool, and before sunset the cliff and cave. A huge brown hairy wild man, naked but for a green wreath, rushes from the pool, drags Ursula from her horse, and raises a club; Richard and Ralph strike him down. Dying, he knows them both: he is the tall champion of the Dry Tree who loved the Lady, never dared enter the cave, and asks to lie beside her. He whispers "The last time!" and dies. The Sage calls him the Dry Tree's great champion and duke. Ursula shears his hair and beard; they wrap him in Ralph's scarlet cloak and bury him beside the Lady's cairn, then ride three miles on by moonlight.
geo: From the shepherd's cot, sheep downs with deep clefts; at noon, the foot of a wide hillside; about an hour later, the stream running to the Swelling Flood from the Lady's bathing pool; before sunset, the cliff and cave, the little green plain, and the sheer bank to the water. Three miles beyond, bushes of the waste.
