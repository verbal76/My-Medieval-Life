summary: The Lady tells of the Land (Kingdom) of the Tower. The old king plotted against her; she and the king's son fled with friends, were caught, and she was tried by bishops for sorcery and condemned to burn. The prince's friends and the townsfolk rescued her; a Bishop with the Host and the Abbot of St. Peter's stopped the battle, and a truce exiled them fifty miles. She was wedded and made Queen, and years of civil war followed: her first son was slain before her and a second died of pestilence. Two years as outlaws in the wildwood brought alliance with the Champions of the Dry Tree (who raided Higham's lands and fought at Bear Castle). After the old king died they took the kingdom; she ruled as "the Woman of the Waste" and swore by the Water of the Well to return to the Dry Tree one day. Her lord built the Castle of Abundance and its arras. Hatred grew; the Teacher of Lore returned young, having drunk of the Well. After her lord died, the Lady fled disguised and lived for years with the Teacher in the House of the Sorceress, learning foresight and dream-sending. She sent the dream to the friar.
geo: The Kingdom of the Tower has a master or great city with the King's House, a Great Square, a city wall with posterns, hill-castles, a town that was the prince's own, and mountain strongholds; it borders the wildwood. The Dry Tree then lived in the wildwood (the Wood Perilous) without a stronghold; they harried north into the Abbot of Higham's lands and over the Downs to Bear Castle. The Lady's party never went north out of the Wood Perilous. The Castle of Abundance was their refuge in the Land of Abundance, which suggests the Land of Abundance was within reach of the Tower lands. The Lady and the Teacher retraced the westward road back to the House of the Sorceress, the Teacher shortening the road by her wisdom.
