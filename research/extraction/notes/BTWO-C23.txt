summary: They camp under elms in the plain and reach Cheaping Knowe before sunset the next day. The town is ringed by cleared ground and strong walls, with a great castle on a hill inside, so the town is a town within a town. Many townsfolk are ragged and hungry; many warriors wear the tyrant's token, a blood-reddened sword. The lord leaves merchants alone for the sake of trade. They lodge at the Fleece. The chapmen bring gifts to the castle and get Ralph a pass as a sergeant of the chapmen's guard; Bull is disguised with docked hair. On the castle hill they see gibbets and impaled men, women and babes. The master church is small and strange; the town gardens have lemons, oranges and pomegranates, with water in stone runnels.
geo: From the foothills a day's ride, with a camp among tall elms in the plain, to Cheaping Knowe. Ground near the walls is cleared of trees. The castle stands high on a hill with the town built around it. A wide market-place holds the best houses.
