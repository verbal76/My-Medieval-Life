summary: They leave Cheaping Knowe, Michael disguised in a wide sallet, and two miles out turn into a deep lane between hazel banks and coppices along the hills, rejoining the highway (the only road into the passes) late in the afternoon. They camp in an oak wood and ride hard the next day. On the third day, above the tilled upland, a dark wood swallows the road; the King of Cheaping Knowe himself waits in the pass mouth with many men, demanding Michael and "the woman". Ralph defies him; as the King threatens to flay Michael and take Ursula, an arrow pierces his throat. Ralph charges crying "Home, home to Upmeads!", his men shout "Ho, for the Friend of the Well!", and arrows and spears from the wood rout the King's men. The bowmen bellow like cattle: the kindred of the Bull. Their chief, wearing a bull's-head helm and gold arm-ring, accepts Bull Shockhead's grass-knot token and leads them toward their dwelling. About sixty dead are left for wolves and kites. They camp at a fell's side and feast.
