summary: At sunrise they ride across country (not by the Greenway) with Giles and twenty-three Garton spearmen. After about two leagues, over a low green ridge, they surprise forty-six Burgers at a cooking fire. Half are killed; the rest surrender. A young captive, promised his life by the sword, says that some Burgers crossed Upmeads Water by the Red Bridge into the Wood Debateable through the Uplands. They made a pact with the wood's strong-thieves to take Upmeads, and this band was sent to bring the southern Burgers. The southern Burgers in the strongholds do not know of the plan. Two thousand are on the road to Upmeads, with more than a thousand expected from Higham-land. Another captive stabs him dead; Stephen cuts the slayer down; Ralph holds to the quarter given. The Garton footmen take the dead men's horses and guard twenty prisoners. They reach Bear Castle two hours before noon among streams of shepherds; Martha has sent round the war-brand. The chant "Smite aside axe, O Bear-father" rises, and smoke and fire go up from the four corners. About two hundred are inside; Giles brings Ralph's men to the north-east corner.
geo: Garton to Bear Castle is reached by mid-morning across country, a shorter road than the Greenway. The ambush dale is about two leagues out, below a low green ridge. The Red Bridge crosses Upmeads Water; the Burgers went from it into the Wood Debateable through the Uplands. Bear Castle has a rampart, a turf bridge and a great square garth.
