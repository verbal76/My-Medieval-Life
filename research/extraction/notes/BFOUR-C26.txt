summary: In Wulstead's market-place Clement says Katherine is on the north gate in war-gear. Ralph and Ursula go to the House of the Austin Canons, walled on the town's west side, with a newly finished church. Old Upmeads carles guard its gate. In the church King Peter and the Dame stand near the Jesus altar. Peter, not recognising Ralph, speaks wryly of being a kinglet with no craft left. The Dame recognises her son outside and embraces him. Ursula says she is a yeoman's daughter, an orphan with one unloving brother, of the Geirings of old time, and that "churl" means "earl", a man. Ralph lists his titles: Lord of the Little Land of Abundance, sworn brother of the Dry Tree, Lord of the Scaur, brother and Warduke of the Shepherds, a Friend of the Well. The Prior feasts them; Ralph tells a little of the lands beyond Whitwall. Ursula means to go to battle with him.
geo: Wulstead has a market-place with Clement's house nearby and a north gate. The House of the Canons is well walled toward the fields on the west of the town and serves as its chief defence on that side. Its church is newly finished, with white pinnacles and a cross on the eastern gable over the dortoir.
