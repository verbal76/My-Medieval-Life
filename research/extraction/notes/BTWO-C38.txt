summary: Walking east up the narrowing dale, Ralph meets Redhead fishing. Redhead warns him never to go to Utterbol: the Lord means him as a toy for his wife and will later geld him, and if Ralph meets the damsel there the Lord, who loves her, will kill her within three days. On the second night after leaving the Dale they will camp near the one good escape pass, through the forest up into the mountains; Redhead will be chief warder of that side. A sage in the wildwood between Utterbol and the Great Mountains has lore of the mountains and the Well, and might change Ralph's very skin to disguise him. Ralph plans to flee, find the sage, and return in disguise to free the damsel.
