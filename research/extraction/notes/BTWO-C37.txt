summary: At the hastily staked lists, with sharp (unblunted) spears, Ralph unhorses a timid man, Redhead, by hauling him from the saddle; wounds Black Anselm through the shoulder; overthrows or hurts six of Utterbol's best; and in three courses with Otter nearly unhorses him. The Lord concedes no one can match him and grants a boon: Ralph asks pardon for Redhead, whom the Lord meant to set up as an archery target, and Otter softens the sentence. Joyce sits beside the Lord as the supposed Queen, and Ralph believes she is the Lady. In her pavilion the jealous Lady reveals that the Lord's beloved new thrall at Utterbol is named Ursula and always wears a pair of beads. The Lord swore on the sword's edge to protect Agatha.
geo: The lists stand in the Dale of the Tower by a turf ridge customary for games. The company stays two more days in "Tower Dale".
open: Is Ursula the damsel of Bourton (the dream named her Dorothea)?
