summary: At the edge of the plain Ralph stops at Netherton church to pray to St. Nicholas and is shoved aside by an armed man wearing the leafless tree, who cries "The first time!" Ralph climbs Bear Hill past ancient chalk carvings to the earthwork of Bear Castle, looks back north, then rides south along the ancient Greenway. Shepherds of the Downs share his wine, tell of the green rider whose appearances bring evil days, and give him a beacon signal and watchword to summon them in need.
geo: The plain ends at the downs; at the foot is the thorp of Netherton beside a stream, with a church and a house of Canons. A white road climbs Bear Hill, whose westward-facing bent bears a chalk tree and two bears, visible from near Wulstead. Bear Castle, an ancient earthwork, crowns the hill; from it Ralph sees the country behind and makes out the Debateable Wood and hills behind it. South the downs roll like waves, treeless, crossed by the ancient green road (the Greenway). A low hill with seven grave-mounds; a meeting about twenty miles short of Higham.
