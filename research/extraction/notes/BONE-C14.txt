summary: At the Flower de Luce Ralph sees the big man in a sallet and white gaberdine (the man of the leafless tree) with a small cloaked bright-eyed companion leave the hall. Over supper townsmen explain the thralls: Wheat-wearer women taken in war, sold at the market-cross while all Wheat-wearer males are slain, and outside the Burg's law. An old burgher tells the Burg's history: conquest by the Wheat-wearers, three generations of thraldom, a Yule uprising, the Forest Lord and the Fair Lady, and the War-smith who made every freeman a warrior. He says the Dry Tree is kept alive as sport and protected by sorcery, and warns Ralph against asking questions. Roger swears to follow Ralph.
