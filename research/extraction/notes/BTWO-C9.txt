summary: In the waning afternoon the Lady leads Ralph on through pine woods toward a desert dwelling where she has stored food. She explains her marriage: the Knight of the Sun pressed her to wed and took her to Sunway, near the Castle of Abundance, where Walter the Black falsely swore she had taught him witchcraft. Cast out, she was found by the Knight's brother, the tall champion, and went with him to Hampton and the Dry Tree, living between Hampton and Abundance since. The Dry Tree has another, older, fierce Queen, mightier than she. The night after Ralph left the Burg she freed a young woman condemned as a spy, the maiden of Bourton, gave her the dead seeker's chaplet, and taught her the way to the Well.
