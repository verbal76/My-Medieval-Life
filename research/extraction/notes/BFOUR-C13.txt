summary: At a thorp where the Swevenham byway leaves the Whitwall highway, they stay at a poor hostel. The Sage chooses to go on to Upmeads with them; Ursula asks him to teach their children. Michael-a-dale leaves for Swevenham; the Sage reads in his palm that he will live long and may come to Upmeads. On a Saturday market day in late May they reach Whitwall, where Richard the Red meets them in Petergate. Blaise is rich and now Lord of the Porte; Richard killed Earl Walter the Black in battle, and his men sued for peace. At the Lamb (Ralph's old hostel) Richard recognises the Sage from his own boyhood and is convinced the Well is real. In private he questions Ralph about Ursula and is glad Ralph has wedded "into the World of living men, and not to a dream of the Land of Fairy". Hearing the tale, Ralph senses trouble at Upmeads and resolves to leave by moonlight; Richard insists on coming, and the Sage arms himself. They leave by the West-gate Bar.
geo: A little thorp where the highway to Whitwall and the byway to Swevenham part, with a poor hostel. Whitwall's streets include Petergate; the hostel of the Lamb; the West-gate Bar.
