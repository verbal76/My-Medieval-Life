summary: In the wood past the House the lovers rest under an oak; Ralph fears loneliness in a long life; a tame robin perches on Ursula's wrist, the wood's creatures being tame. After three days the wood ends in a stony waste in wave-like ridges, to be crossed at their highest points, covered with salt scurf and small sage bushes. On the second day they find a dried, unwounded armoured corpse, horses, and a woman who stabbed herself, and bury them under stones; then more dead. On the third day there are too many to bury. Ursula notes none wear the seekers' beads, the dead are from many ages, and the way-tokens still show; they go on.
