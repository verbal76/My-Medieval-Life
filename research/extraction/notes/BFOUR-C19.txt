summary: Roger and Stephen bring fifty-five champions of the Dry Tree, granted by the Burg's captain, on condition that Ralph swear at the Castle of the Scaur, with Ursula on the Lady's high-seat, to help the Burg and the Wheat-wearers when called. They ride out by the North Gate through the Freedom of the Burg, through Grey Goose Thicket (once an ambush place), to a ridge above a green plain: a great reef of grey rocks with the white Castle of the Scaur and a huge round crown-tower, and the thorp of Hampton below. Michael a-Hurst says the old Burgers have armed and come into the woodland north of the Wood Debateable; the Abbot of Higham's recent foes are the Banded Barons from the West. Ursula fears the hall where the old she-bear Queen condemned her; Roger says he killed that Queen there after the Lady's death. In the vast empty hall Roger blows a great horn to hallow the Mote; Ursula sits on the stone high-seat; Ralph swears on his sword and her head; the champions swear to follow him a year and a day, and offer him the Scaur and Hampton if he proves himself. Ten score Dry Tree riders arrive with the new Captain of the Dry Tree, who says the expelled Burgers in the Wood Debateable plan to attack Upmeads, then Higham, then the Burg. He leaves a hundred to hold the Scaur, gives Ralph a hundred, and says five hundred Wheat-wearers are coming with remounts. Ralph accepts the Scaur if he survives. They ride for Higham by the shortest by-ways.
geo: From the Burg's North Gate across the Freedom of the Burg, off the woodland highway, along forest rides to Grey Goose Thicket (narrow, single-file), then a valley trench to a bare ridge overlooking a green plain with a great reef of grey rocks crowned by the white Castle of the Scaur and its great round tower; the thorp of Hampton (white thatched houses) at the ridge foot; a walled stair from the bailey up the rock to the hall level. Higham is "no great way hence". The old Burgers are now in the woodland north of the Wood Debateable.
