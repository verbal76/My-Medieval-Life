summary: The pass continues the next day; they camp by water running from the rocks. Ursula tells of her home: her parents, a sister who died, her brother, and suitors. Her lover at Bourton faked his death to join the Burg's riders, being weary of her; she already knew she loved him little. For many days the pass opens into small grassy valleys with water and trees, then becomes one high valley, "a trench of the great mountain", and they find the Sage's tokens throughout. Thirty days after leaving the Sage, as days shorten, they descend a rugged neck into a wide valley with a river and great groves of sweet chestnut and walnut, half split by a rock reef from the northern mountain. They ford the river and find at the reef's head a cavern marked with the sword-and-branch token: their winter house. They gather and dry chestnuts into meal, gather walnuts, hunt deer and bears (Ralph kills two), and Ursula sews furs.
geo: The pass of the Fighting Man runs for many days, first a narrow cleft, then opening into small grassy valleys and finally one long high valley. After thirty days from the Sage: the Vale of Sweet Chestnuts, a much wider valley with a great rock reef thrusting from the high mountain and nearly cleaving its northern half, a fair river with a ford, and groves of chestnuts and walnuts on both banks. The cavern is in the face of the reef at its head, with a low creeping entry and a high clean rock-hall inside.
